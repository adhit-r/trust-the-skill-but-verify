# Data Retention Policy

Survey data and project records are kept only as long as a client contract or the law requires.

## Retention Periods

1. Raw survey data: three years after project close.
2. Signed reports: seven years.
3. Visit logs: two years.
4. Timesheets: six years.

## Disposal

The data steward approves disposal each January. Disposal is recorded in the retention register with the date and method.

#### Legal Hold

A legal hold from the legal team overrides any retention period listed above until it is lifted in writing.
