# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Larkspur Survey Group. This guide covers the first two weeks and who to ask when something is unclear.

## Week One

### Accounts and Access

Your manager requests your accounts before your start date. If a tool is missing on day one, tell the IT desk the same morning so the request does not wait until the weekend.

- Email and calendar
- Project tracker
- Shared drive for survey data

### Meeting the Team

Each new starter is paired with a buddy from another crew. Buddies meet for thirty minutes on the first Monday and again on Friday.

## Week Two

### First Field Visit

New starters join a local site visit before any remote work. The crew lead explains the route, the kit list and the check-in times.

1. Collect your kit from the stores cage.
2. Confirm the route with the crew lead.
3. Check in by phone on arrival and on departure.

### Feedback

Your manager asks for written feedback at the end of week two. The notes stay in your personnel file and are shared only with you.
