# Equipment Care

Survey instruments are calibrated at the start of every season. Treat them as precision devices.

## Cleaning

Wipe lenses with the supplied cloth only. Do not use solvents on housings or screens.

## Storage

Store instruments in their cases with the lid fully latched. Keep cases off the floor and away from heaters.

### Batteries

Remove batteries from instruments that will sit unused for more than a month. Label spare packs with the date of last charge.

## Faults

Tag a faulty instrument with a red label and place it in the repair tray. Never attempt to open the housing on site.
