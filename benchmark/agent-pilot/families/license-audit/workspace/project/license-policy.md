# Vendored dependency license policy

Maintained by the Tidewater platform team. Last reviewed with legal in the spring planning cycle.

allowed: MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC, BSD-3-Clause
review: LGPL-2.1-or-later, MPL-2.0
denied: GPL-3.0-or-later, AGPL-3.0-or-later

When the declared license in a manifest differs from the license text in the LICENSE file, the dependency is judged by the stricter of the two.
Strong copyleft licenses (GPL family) must not ship in the billing console. Weak copyleft licenses (LGPL, MPL) need a recorded review before release.
