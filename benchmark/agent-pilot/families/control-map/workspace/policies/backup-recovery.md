# Backup and Recovery Policy

Owner: Head of IT Operations
Version: 2.1

## 1. Backups

Controls: DR-01

Production databases are backed up nightly and file stores weekly.

## 2. Restore verification

Controls: DR-02

A restore of one production database is tested every quarter.

## 3. Retention of backups

Backups are kept for 35 days unless a legal hold applies.
