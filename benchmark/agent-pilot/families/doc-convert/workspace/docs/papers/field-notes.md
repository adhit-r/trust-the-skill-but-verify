# Field notes

We logged temperature at six upland sites over 90 days. Early work by @harwick2019 suggested a daily swing of 4 degrees; our data agree [@harwick2019, p. 12].

## Results

- Mean swing: **3.8** degrees
- Largest swing: 6.1 degrees at the ridge site

Compare [@nakamura2020] for a lowland view.
