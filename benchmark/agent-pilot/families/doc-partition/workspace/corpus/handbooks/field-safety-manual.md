# Field Safety Manual

This manual applies to every crew working outside the office. Read it before your first site visit.

## Before You Leave

Plan the route and tell the duty officer where you are going. Carry a charged phone and a spare battery pack.

### Personal Protective Equipment

Wear high-visibility clothing and sturdy boots on every site. Hard hats are required near plant or scaffolding.

- High-visibility vest
- Safety boots
- Hard hat
- Gloves

### Weather Checks

Check the forecast the evening before and again before departure. Cancel the visit when the wind warning is amber or higher.

## On Site

Walk the area before you start measuring. Report any hazard to the crew lead and record it in the visit log.

### Working Near Water

Never work alone within five metres of open water. Keep one person on the bank at all times.

### Lone Working

Lone working is allowed only on sites rated low risk. Check in every hour; the duty officer escalates after two missed check-ins.

## After the Visit

1. Return all kit to the stores cage.
2. Report damaged equipment the same day.
3. Complete the visit log before leaving.
