# Deploying loggers

Choose a spot that is shaded and away from standing water.

> Never mount a unit on a metal fence post during a storm.

## Mounting

```
bracket --height 1.5m --tilt 0
```

Placement follows the method in [@harwick2019] and the drift limits from [@osei2021; @lindgren2022].
