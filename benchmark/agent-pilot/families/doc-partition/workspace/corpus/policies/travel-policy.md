# Travel Policy

Staff may book travel that is needed for site visits, client meetings and approved training.

## Booking

Book rail or economy flights at least fourteen days ahead where possible. Hotel bookings go through the travel desk.

### Approval

- Trips under 200 km: line manager approval.
- Trips over 200 km: line manager and finance approval.
- Overnight stays abroad: director approval.

## Expenses

Submit receipts within thirty days of returning. Claims without receipts are returned to the claimant.

### Mileage

Mileage on a personal vehicle is paid at the rate on the finance intranet page. Record start and end readings in the claim.
